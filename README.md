## My Favorite TV Series: Real Steel
---

### Overview
---

**Real Steel** is a 2011 American **science fiction sports** film starring Hugh Jackman. Produced and directed by Shawn Levy, the film is based on the short story "Steel", written by Richard Matheson, which was originally published in the May 1956 edition of The Magazine of Fantasy & Science Fiction, and later adapted into a 1963 Twilight Zone episode.

### Main Characters
---

1. **Hugh Jackman** A former boxer (Jackman), whose sport is now played by robots, as he and his son (Dakota Goyo) find an abandoned robot and train it to be a promising fighter.
2. **Dakota Avery Goyo** (born August 22, 1999) is a Canadian former actor. He is best known for playing **Max Kenton** in the film Real Steel.
